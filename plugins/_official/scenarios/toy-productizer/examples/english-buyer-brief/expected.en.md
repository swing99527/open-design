# Retro Space Robot Gift Shop Proposal

## 1. Customer / Task Recap

A US museum buyer wants a retro space robot collectible for the gift shop. The product should feel educational, display easily near checkout, stay under a $15 retail price, and avoid copying the reference robot silhouette.

## 2. Creative Direction Comparison

| Direction | Product Form | Buyer Fit | Differentiation | Key Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Retro Science Robot Mini | Small collectible figure with backer card | Strong museum-gift fit and easy checkout display | New body proportions, exhibit-label graphics, and science mission details instead of the reference silhouette | Cost and size must stay controlled for the price target |
| B. Space Lab Desk Token | Flat display token with robot relief | Simple packaging and strong educational message | Shifts from character copy to lab artifact language | May feel less premium than a small figure |
| C. Mission Crew Mini Lineup | Three mini robots by science theme | Creates a collectible series and gift-shop shelf story | Each SKU uses a different science role and silhouette | More SKUs increase development complexity |

## 3. Recommended Direction And Rationale

Recommended direction: Retro Science Robot Mini.

This direction best balances museum relevance, checkout display, and collectible appeal. It keeps the retro space robot mood while changing the silhouette, proportions, face language, accessories, and packaging identity away from the reference.

## 4. Product Story

The Retro Science Robot Mini is a small museum-shop collectible inspired by early space exploration exhibits. It presents the robot as a friendly "science guide" rather than a copied character, using mission labels, star-map packaging, and educational micro-copy to connect the product to museum learning.

## 5. SKU / Lineup

- Core SKU: Retro Science Robot Mini
- Optional lineup: Moon Lab, Observatory, Rocket Bay
- Display format: compact backer card or small window box for checkout counter racks
- Unconfirmed assumption: retail price target stays under USD 15

- Packaging: exhibit-label backer card with mission number, short science note, and buyer-friendly product story
- Display: peg hook, counter tray, or small shelf-ready carton
